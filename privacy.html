Privacy & your study data

Updated 4 November 2026

What the app stores

When you sign in, Study Studio uses your account identifier and email address to open your private workspace. Your workspace includes the profiles, subjects, credits, units, topics, preparation statuses, assessments and grade settings that you enter.

Where it is stored

Signed-in workspaces are stored in Supabase with account-based access policies. Authentication sessions are stored in your browser so you can stay signed in. Unsynced account edits may be retained in this browser tab until they are saved. Guest work is saved in this browser only and is not uploaded automatically. Exported backups contain your study data; keep them private.

Services involved

Vercel hosts the site. Supabase handles accounts and saved workspaces. Google or GitHub handles authentication when you choose that sign-in method. Brevo delivers account confirmation and password-reset emails. These providers process data needed for their services under their own privacy policies. Study Studio does not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail messages or GitHub repositories for signing in.

Your choices

You can edit your study data, export a backup, and remove profiles, subjects or units through Manage workspace. Changes to your signed-in workspace are saved online. Signing out returns to this browser’s guest workspace. Clearing browser storage removes guest work and local sessions but does not delete your saved account workspace.
